About Us

Welcome to Espresso

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Espresso was created with a passion for exceptional coffee, fresh pastries, and memorable experiences.

What began as a single neighborhood café has grown into a beloved destination for coffee enthusiasts and casual visitors alike. Today, we proudly serve our communities across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, and Westchester.

With more than 20 cafés and our own roastery in Long Island City, we remain committed to quality, consistency, and genuine hospitality in every cup.

The Espresso Experience

Why Choose Us?

Espresso offers more than just great coffee. We create a welcoming space where you can relax, connect, and enjoy a carefully crafted beverage made just for you.

  • Fresh coffee roasted in-house
  • Professionally trained baristas
  • Classic coffees and signature beverages
  • House-made sauces and syrups
  • Natural, high-quality ingredients
  • Fresh pastries made by our in-house baker

We look forward to welcoming you to Espresso.
